Connecting Classrooms Through the Power of Art

We match US schools with partner classrooms around the world, turning art supply donations into lasting creative connections.

How It Works

A simple four-step process to connect classrooms across the globe

1

Sign Up

US and Global South schools complete a quick registration form sharing grade levels, interests, and needs.

2

Get Matched

We pair your classroom with a partner school based on grade level and artistic interests.

3

Connect & Create

Message your partner classroom, share photos of artwork, and build real connections.

4

Share Impact

See the direct impact of donations as partner students share what they create with your supplies.
